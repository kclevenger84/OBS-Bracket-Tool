Enter the teams in seed order: seed 1 is the best team. The bracket is drawn from the seeds (with 8 teams: 1 v 8, 4 v 5, 2 v 7, 3 v 6), so the top seeds can only meet late. If the number of teams isn't 2, 4, 8, 16 or 32, the top seeds skip the first round. Upload a logo or drop image files into the logos folder next to the server.
